GOOD TO KNOW

A few answers.

How do I get a quote? +

Add the items you’re interested in, complete the event details and prepare your enquiry through WhatsApp. We’ll follow up to discuss availability and pricing.

Are the listed prices final? +

Prices are on request. Your quote depends on the items, quantities, event details and availability.

Does sending an enquiry confirm my booking? +

No. An enquiry is a request for information, not a confirmed booking. Your booking is confirmed only after arrangements are agreed with GROWTH.

Can I ask about delivery or setup? +

Yes. Include delivery, setup or collection requirements in the enquiry and GROWTH can confirm what is available for your event.
